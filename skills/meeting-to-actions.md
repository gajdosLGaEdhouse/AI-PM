# Skill Contract: Meeting Notes to Actions and Decisions

## Purpose
Convert meeting notes or transcripts into an actionable record: summary, action items, decisions, risks, and impacted artifacts.

## When to use
Use after workshops, status meetings, planning sessions, demos, retrospectives, or customer calls.

## Primary methodology coverage
- 04-01 Ceremonies
- 04-04 Monitoring
- 04-05 Customer reporting

## Required inputs
- transcript or meeting notes,
- optional attendee list and agenda.

## Output artifacts
- `artifacts/meeting-notes/<date>-<meeting-name>.md`
- suggested updates to decision log, risk register, or roadmap

## Guardrails
- do not infer decisions where there was only discussion,
- preserve unresolved questions,
- keep changed-artifact suggestions explicit.
