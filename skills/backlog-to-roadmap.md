# Skill Contract: Backlog to Roadmap

## Purpose
Translate backlog, dependencies, and staffing assumptions into a realistic first-pass `artifacts/roadmap.md`.

## When to use
Use after a backlog or WBS exists and the project needs milestone planning or a roadmap revision.

## Primary methodology coverage
- 03-03 Scope and estimates
- 04-04 Monitoring
- 04-05 Customer reporting

## Required inputs
- backlog or WBS,
- staffing or capacity assumptions,
- milestone targets if available.

## Output artifacts
- `artifacts/roadmap.md`
- schedule-risk notes
- milestone confidence notes

## Guardrails
- do not treat aspirational dates as commitments,
- show uncertainty explicitly,
- include testing and stabilization space.
