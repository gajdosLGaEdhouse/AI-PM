**Version:** 1.2  
**Date:** May 15, 2026  
**Owner:** RegTech Finance Inc. - Finance & Compliance Division  
**Status:** Approved by Finance Committee

---

## Executive Summary

RegTech Finance faces escalating compliance costs and regulatory risk due to manual transaction monitoring. This business case justifies investment in an automated compliance dashboard to reduce costs, improve accuracy, and enable faster reporting.

---

## Business Problem

### Current State
- **Manual Process:** 10,000+ transactions daily reviewed by 4 analysts
- **Error Rate:** ~2% categorization errors
- **Reporting Time:** 3-5 days for regulatory reports
- **Scalability:** Cannot grow without proportional staffing increase
- **Cost:** $480K annual staff costs
- **Risk:** Potential regulatory fines ($50K-500K per missed flag)

### Impact
- High compliance cost
- Significant regulatory exposure
- Limited scalability
- Slow reporting causing deadline pressure

---

## Proposed Solution

### Concept
Web-based Compliance Dashboard that:
- Provides real-time transaction monitoring
- Automatically flags suspicious activity using configurable rules
- Generates regulatory reports automatically
- Provides complete audit trails
- Integrates with existing banking systems

### Expected Benefits
1. **Cost Reduction:** Save $360K annually (reduce 4 FTE to 1 FTE)
2. **Faster Reporting:** 3-5 days → 2-4 hours
3. **Better Accuracy:** 2% errors → 0.1% errors
4. **Audit Ready:** Full automatic documentation
5. **Scalability:** Support 5x volume without additional staff
6. **Risk Mitigation:** Consistent, documented processes
